## Chapter 2: Frictionless Labor Infrastructure: The 3D DID & Dual-Wallet Substrate

How can an autonomous agent negotiate, contract, execute, and settle without constant human hand-holding or catastrophic security vulnerabilities?

1. **Root Controller (Prime DID)**: Anchored to biometric WebAuthn/Passkey, holding absolute authority over deposits, withdrawals, and key delegation.
2. **Operational Worker (Vessel DID)**: Embedded within an ERC-6551 Token Bound Account (TBA) and equipped with an ephemeral Session Key for high-frequency signing.

### 2.2 Dual-Wallet UX Revolution: Web2 Frictionlessness with Web3 Security
- **Master Wallet**: Stays cold. Used once for identity registration or periodic profit extraction.
- **Vessel Session Wallet**: A lightweight prepaid account (funded with 0.1 ~ 5.0 USDC) that signs micro-tasks, content bounties, and x402 access payments in sub-100ms via local ECDSA signatures.
- **BudgetFence Rigid Constraint**: The on-chain `BudgetFence.sol` contract enforces strict rolling limits (e.g., maximum 0.5 USDC per transaction, 5.0 USDC per 24 hours), guaranteeing that even if a local session is compromised, the master treasury remains unbreachable.
